Everything is installed fresh from the official sources. Your profile's settings are applied on top. Nothing is copied from an old install.

What happens

  • Decky Loader, then every plugin in your profile from the Decky store, verified by checksum.
  • CSS Loader themes and sound packs from the deckthemes store, with your enabled themes and patch choices restored.
  • Firefox with your extensions, GE-Proton, CryoUtilities, SSH and the Desktop Mode tweaks you pick next.
  • Safe to run again later, for example after a SteamOS update removes Decky.
